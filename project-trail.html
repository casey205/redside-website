Project Overview

Walking the Trail Before It Exists

Reality capture of a proposed trail corridor creates a shared spatial understanding that drawings and maps alone can't provide.

Trail alignment decisions involve terrain, vegetation, drainage, and experience factors that traditional plan and profile drawings just don't communicate well. A 3D corridor model changes that. Everyone involved can explore the proposed route, see where the grades get steep, spot the potential problem areas, and ask better questions.

For recreation organizations and trail builders, that means fewer surprises once construction starts, better design decisions earlier, and stakeholder conversations that actually move things forward.

"The ability to walk a trail virtually before construction and share that experience with project reviewers and community stakeholders changes the conversation entirely."

Applications

  • Recreation planning and mountain bike trail design
  • Land trusts, conservancies, and permit review support
  • Community and stakeholder engagement
  • Environmental and permit review support
  • Volunteer and crew orientation

Why This Matters
