Project Overview

Existing Conditions, Captured Once

A complete, measurable 3D model of a complex structure, available to every project team member without another site visit.

Complex buildings are hard to measure with conventional methods: irregular geometry, tight access, multi-level structures, things you can't easily get to. Reality capture handles this by producing a complete, navigable 3D model from a single visit.

Engineers, architects, and facility managers can all work from that model remotely, measuring, reviewing, and planning without having to schedule another trip to the building.

"Capturing the building once and making that data available to the entire team eliminates the inefficiency of multiple site visits for different disciplines."

Applications

  • Renovation design and construction planning
  • Facility management and maintenance planning
  • Historic preservation documentation
  • Remote design review by distributed teams
  • Construction verification and as-built records
