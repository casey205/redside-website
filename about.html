The Background

Grounded in Surveying. Driven by Terrain.

There's a specific kind of understanding that comes from being a land surveyor: a precision in how you think about the landscape and a discipline around accuracy that shapes everything else.

Redside Surveying & Mapping was built on that foundation. The surveying background isn't incidental. It's the reason the data is trustworthy, the coordinate systems are correct, and GNSS control is planned and executed with the care the rest of the project will depend on.

GNSS is a core strength, not a checkbox. Deep field proficiency, experience setting project control in demanding terrain, and a track record of leading industry workshops on GNSS practice for surveying and geospatial teams.

Beyond the technical credentials, there's a genuine interest in the landscape: terrain, trails, rivers, and the ways people work in and around them. That combination of technical rigor and real interest in the subject matter shapes what this practice does and how it does it.

Capabilities

What We Bring to a Project

A combination of credentials, tools, and genuine expertise, not a list of software licenses.

Surveying & GNSS

Licensed Professional Land Surveyor with strong GNSS expertise: project control, field workflows, and accuracy you can document. Known in the industry for leading GNSS workshops and helping teams get reliable results in the field.

GIS Expertise

Deep experience in spatial analysis, terrain modeling, and custom geospatial workflows, including QGIS, coordinate systems, and raster analysis.

Reality Capture

3D mesh models, orthomosaics, and terrain data from aerial and ground-based capture, processed and delivered in formats that are actually usable.

Trail Planning

Specific experience in trail design visualization and corridor analysis, including custom QGIS plugins and workflows built for terrain-intensive planning work.

Restoration Support

Understanding of how terrain data applies to watershed and habitat restoration: site context, before/after documentation, and remote review support.

Digital Delivery

Web-based project communication tools that make geospatial data accessible to everyone on a project, not just GIS specialists.

Our Approach

The Technology Serves the Understanding

We deliver surveying, reality capture, GIS, and interactive project delivery so clients understand places clearly and can act with confidence.

The tools (LiDAR, photogrammetry, GIS, 3D modeling) are means to an end. The end is a client who can walk into a meeting, review a permit application, plan a restoration strategy, or make a design decision with real confidence about what the site actually looks like.

That orientation shapes how we scope projects, what deliverables we recommend, and how we communicate results. We start with what you need to understand, then figure out the right way to produce it.
