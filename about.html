About Redside

A surveying and geospatial consulting practice focused on helping clients better understand landscapes and infrastructure through modern mapping technologies.

The Background

Grounded in Surveying. Driven by Terrain.

I started Redside because I kept finding myself at the overlap of two things I genuinely care about: precise spatial data and the landscapes that data describes.

My background is in land surveying, and that's not incidental to what Redside does. It's the foundation. Surveying teaches you to be honest about accuracy, careful about coordinate systems, and clear-eyed about the difference between data that looks right and data you can actually build on. That distinction shapes every project I take on.

GNSS is where I've spent a lot of focused time: field workflows, project control, benchmark networks, the kind of consistent spatial framework across a site that lets LiDAR, GIS, and construction data all reference the same thing. I've led industry workshops on GNSS practice and find that work genuinely interesting. Helping teams get reliable results in demanding terrain is a real problem worth solving.

Bend is home base. The work takes me across the Pacific Northwest, and the projects I find most interesting tend to involve complex terrain and people doing meaningful work in it. Trails, rivers, restoration sites, places where the quality of the spatial data actually changes what's possible.

Capabilities

What We Bring to a Project

What I actually bring to a project. Not a spec sheet of software licenses.

Surveying & GNSS

Licensed PLS with deep GNSS experience: project control, field workflows, and accuracy you can cite. I've led industry workshops on GNSS practice and spend a lot of time thinking about how to get reliable results in difficult terrain.

GIS Expertise

Deep experience in spatial analysis, terrain modeling, and custom geospatial workflows, including QGIS, coordinate systems, and raster analysis.

Reality Capture

3D mesh models, orthomosaics, and terrain data from aerial and ground capture. Processed and handed off in formats your team can open and use, not just look at.

Trail Planning

Corridor analysis, alignment visualization, and custom QGIS tools built around the specific decisions trail designers need to make.

Restoration Support

Understanding of how terrain data applies to watershed and habitat restoration: site context, before/after documentation, and remote review support.

Digital Delivery

Project data shared via a browser link so everyone on the team can actually see what's going on, not just the GIS person.

Our Approach

The Tools Are a Means to an End

LiDAR, photogrammetry, GIS, 3D models. All of it exists to help a client walk into a meeting and actually know what their site looks like.

The end goal is a client who can review a permit application, plan a restoration strategy, or make a design decision with real confidence about the ground truth. Not a client who received a file they're not sure how to open.

That shapes how I scope work, what I recommend delivering, and how I talk about results. I start with what you need to understand, then figure out the right way to produce it.

"Good data changes what's possible. When a trail designer can actually see the terrain before flagging, or a restoration team can review a site without driving three hours, the quality of decisions goes up. That's what I'm trying to deliver."
