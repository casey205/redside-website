Custom Geospatial Workflows

Terrain Intelligence Built Around Your Work

Custom plugins, MTB Design Tools workspaces, and processing pipelines built for trail design, restoration, and infrastructure decisions.

A typical trail workflow: design and analyze in MTB Design Tools, deliver mapping products to CalTopo for planning and field coordination, then publish the corridor in NIRA for interactive stakeholder review.

See How It Works
1

Design & Analyze. Custom workspace and terrain tools (including MTB Design Tools) tuned to your corridor and site questions.

2

Deliver to CalTopo. Clean linework, surfaces, and context layers formatted for planning and field use.

3

Publish in NIRA. Interactive 3D models for remote review, alignment walkthroughs, and stakeholder buy-in.

4

Survey-Grade Foundation. Licensed surveying, control, and QA/QC so the data behind the workflow holds up in design.
