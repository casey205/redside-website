Trail Planning · Recreation

Trail Corridor Visualization

Reality capture of a proposed trail corridor so planners, designers, and stakeholders can walk the alignment virtually and understand terrain, sight lines, and design challenges before anyone picks up a shovel.

Especially useful for mountain bike trail designers, recreation organizations, and conservation groups trying to build support for new alignments.

Watershed Restoration · Environmental

Watershed Restoration Site

A detailed terrain model of a watershed restoration area that gives environmental consultants and restoration teams accurate site context without everyone needing to drive out there first.

Useful for restoration planning, regulatory review, habitat assessment, and keeping a distributed project team on the same page.

Existing Conditions · Infrastructure

Complex Building Model

Detailed reality capture of a structurally complex building for existing conditions documentation. The model captures geometry that's hard to measure conventionally, supporting renovation design and facility planning.

Useful for engineering firms, architects, and facilities teams that need accurate as-built records without having to schedule repeated site access.
