How I Work

Services

Technology is never the starting point. I start with what your team needs to understand, then figure out the right way to capture, analyze, and deliver it.

Understand Your Site
Accurate terrain data and 3D documentation before decisions get made on assumptions
  • 3D mesh models and terrain capture via UAS and ground methods
  • Orthomosaics and georeferenced aerial imagery
  • Existing conditions documentation for buildings, sites, and corridors
  • Before and after capture for monitoring and change detection
  • Remote site orientation so your team can understand the site without driving out
Plan With Confidence
Custom tools and workflows built around the decisions your project actually needs to make
  • Terrain modeling, slope, aspect, and drainage analysis
  • Corridor analysis and trail alignment review
  • Watershed delineation and hydrology mapping
  • Custom QGIS workflows and plugins tailored to your project type
  • Mapping products and materials built for your stakeholders, not just GIS users
Keep Everyone Aligned
Deliverables anyone on the team can open, understand, and use
  • Interactive 3D project visualization anyone can open in a browser
  • Shareable project links for distributed teams and remote reviewers
  • Web-based site communication for stakeholder engagement
  • Report graphics and presentation materials
Build a System
Data and workflows that hold up over time and improve with each project
  • Topographic and existing conditions surveys
  • GNSS project control and benchmark networks
  • Georeferenced LiDAR, ortho, and 3D deliverables tied to survey control
  • QA/QC of terrain and remote sensing deliverables
  • Accuracy documentation engineers and design teams can rely on
The Difference

Surveying is in the foundation

I'm a licensed surveyor. That shapes how I think about data: what accurate actually means, and why it matters downstream.

Surveying trains you to care about things that aren't always visible in the output: coordinate systems, control networks, accuracy documentation. The kind of details that seem invisible until an engineer tries to use the data six months later and needs it to hold up.

I do topographic survey, spatial control, and QA/QC on remote sensing deliverables because that foundation matters. Not as a credential to display, but because it changes what I notice and what I check.

GNSS & Project Control Deep GNSS experience: benchmarks, control networks, and the consistent spatial framework that lets LiDAR, GIS, and construction all reference the same ground. I lead industry workshops on this because it's genuinely interesting work.
Coordinate Systems Done Right The right datum, projection, and coordinate system from the start, so everything integrates cleanly and nothing has to be untangled later.
Collected with the End in Mind Knowing how terrain data gets used downstream in design, analysis, and construction shapes how I collect it. What gets captured and how it gets documented isn't an afterthought.
