The Difference

Surveying is in the foundation

Licensed Professional Land Surveyor credentials aren't a footnote. They're the basis for data you can trust.

Many geospatial providers deliver visuals that look right. Redside focuses on datasets that are survey-controlled and ready for design: proper accuracy standards, coordinate systems, project control, and documentation your engineers and consultants can actually build on.

Topographic survey and spatial control are core. Boundary and legal surveys are not our focus. Remote sensing QA/QC and deliverable review are a major part of how we help clients avoid expensive mistakes on bad data.

Custom Geospatial Workflows

Terrain Intelligence,
Built to Fit Your Work

Off-the-shelf tools solve general problems. When the analysis is specific, whether it's a particular landscape, discipline, or decision, a custom workflow is often the right answer.

We develop custom plugins, processing scripts, and analysis workflows in QGIS and other geospatial environments, built around the actual decisions your team needs to make, not generic tool palettes.

An example from trail design work: a custom terrain analysis workflow that takes LiDAR or reality capture data and immediately surfaces the information a trail designer actually needs: slope distribution along a corridor, drainage crossings, grade profiles, without manual setup for every project.

The result isn't a product. It's a capability: a way of working that removes friction from the analysis so more time goes into the decisions that matter.
